# Karachi Food Pages — MVP build packet

**Purpose:** a strict product, design, and engineering brief for a small public website that presents food-business website concepts for Karachi and routes interested owners to one configured contact channel.

**Working name only:** “Karachi Food Pages” is a placeholder, not an approved brand.

## Read in this order

1. `BUILD-RULES.md` — instructions for any coding agent; scope and source-of-truth rules.
2. `docs/PRD.md` — who it serves, what the MVP does, what it deliberately does not do, and acceptance criteria.
3. `docs/DESIGN.md` — visual system, page hierarchy, the center “round table” gallery, and responsive behavior.
4. `docs/TRD-ARCHITECTURE.md` — recommended stack, routes, data contract, component boundaries, and verification plan.
5. `docs/OWNER-DECISIONS.md` — unresolved inputs and release blockers.
6. `.agents/skills/*/SKILL.md` — focused workflows for scope, food-sector pages, the gallery, and release checks.

`CLAUDE.md` and `.github/copilot-instructions.md` point agents to `BUILD-RULES.md`. Keep all four skills with the project when copying this packet into a codebase.

## Product decision this packet assumes

This is a **lead-generation showcase**, not a restaurant directory, ordering marketplace, or website builder. Visitors browse five clearly fictional sample page designs; local business owners can request a similar website. The app itself does not take food orders.

## Before public launch

The owner must choose the final brand/domain, confirm the five sectors, provide the real sales/contact destination, and either approve the sample content/assets or replace them with licensed, business-approved material. Unconfirmed values are marked as decisions or launch gates; do not invent them.
